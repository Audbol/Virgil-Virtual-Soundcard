Virgil is installed

Select Virgil Virtual Soundcard in System Settings > Sound or in your audio app.

Open Virgil Control (in Applications; it lives in the menu bar) to pick the network interface on your Dante network and change the device name, channels and latency. Route channels in Dante Controller.
